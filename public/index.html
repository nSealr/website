non-profit open-source signer program

NostrSeal

A reproducible ecosystem for Nostr signing devices: shared specs, a desktop companion, Raspberry/Pi stateless QR vaults, ESP32 firmware, smartcard research, custom hardware-wallet research, and open hardware references.

Specs Companion Research Lab

product shape

One companion, multiple signer lines.

The companion prepares requests, moves them over QR, file, stdio, serial, USB, or card transports, and verifies every successful response. The signer keeps or protects the private key and signs only through an explicit policy boundary.

Raspberry/Pi Stateless QR Vault

Air-gapped RAM-only Raspberry/Pi flow inspired by SeedSigner principles for Nostr events.

ESP32

USB/NIP-46 and stateless QR firmware lines for ESP32-S3 first, with classic ESP32 compatibility later.

Smartcard

Display-less APDU card research for key protection, not trusted event review alone.

status

Current maturity is explicit.

  • Specs: v0 request, response, QR envelope, fixtures, review-screen vectors, and approval_digest contracts.
  • Companion: CLI, verification, transports, QR, serial framing.
  • Raspberry: desktop stateless QR vault simulation with NIP-06 signing and approval_digest-bound review.
  • ESP32: native USB scaffold with get_public_key, signing_disabled, approval_digest-gated approval core, and ESP32 stateless QR vault host-core review flow.
  • Smartcard: APDU simulator for public key and event-id signing.
  • Hardware: checked requirements and BOM scaffold, including request-id and approval_digest review binding.

security

No production security claim yet.

NostrSeal is pre-production research and implementation work. The project documents trust boundaries, test evidence, and known limits before presenting any device as ready for real keys.
