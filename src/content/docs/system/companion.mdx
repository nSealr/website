Bridge connects clients to the selected signer and verifies the response for
the separately expected identity. The normative device engine is Rust; host
transport code does not approve signatures or hold the user's identity key.
NIP-46 client/transport keys have a distinct bounded lifecycle.

## Current source

The [consolidated product](https://github.com/nSealr/nsealr) owns:

- `apps/bridge/cli` and `apps/bridge/service`;
- client, protocol, policy, QR and transport modules in `packages`;
- browser-provider and extension integration;
- `packages/sdk`, with browser and Node entry points;
- the authoritative `protocols/contracts/v1` contracts and vectors.

The old companion/specs/raspberry repositories retain migration history.
Use the [public development quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart/);
no private lab or legacy sibling checkout is required.

## Verified development behavior and remaining gates

The S3 public-fixture flow has exercised a real CLI/client/service/USB path,
physical approval and rejection, and cryptographically verified output.
That evidence does not qualify general session custody, entropy, recovery,
every client, or production release installation.

P4 manual QR host wiring is still an explicitly bounded public experiment
awaiting its full source/artifact and optical workflow qualification. Its
future browser/manual native-host integration is not implied by the Node path.

Methods and transport availability remain profile-specific. Generic metadata
must not turn a conformance fixture into an available device, and host previews
cannot stand in for complete physical pages or fresh consent. Unsupported
automation or offline messaging must be refused explicitly.

The [current product documentation](https://github.com/nSealr/nsealr/tree/main/docs/bridge)
and [task graph](https://github.com/nSealr/nsealr/blob/main/plan/work-items.json)
carry exact source, version, admission and evidence limits. No global browser,
npm, NIP-46 client or device release compatibility claim follows from a helper
being exported.
