## What you need

Use three independent devices and one [companion](/docs/clients/). Setup creates
a new Nostr identity. Each device holds a different private share; any two
members can sign. Keep each share and its backup in a separate safe place.

A group supports public-key requests and event signatures. Use a normal identity
created from recovery words or an nsec for NIP-44 encryption and private messages.

## Create the group

1. On each device, choose **Create identity → 2-of-3 signing group → Start group**.
2. In the companion, choose **Create group**. Scan all three public join codes.
   Choose **Captured** on each device when its code has arrived.
3. Scan the companion's setup request on all three devices. Compare the complete
   setup fingerprint on every device, then approve on each.
4. Each device shows two encrypted packets. Scan both into the companion, using
   **Captured** to move to the next packet. Collect all six before forwarding any.
5. The companion names the device that must receive each packet. Scan it on that
   device and check its progress before choosing **Next packet** in the companion.
   That button records your progress; it is not an acknowledgement from the device.
6. Repeat collection and forwarding for the second set of six packets. Finally,
   scan the public group record from each device. All three must agree.

At **Group ready**, choose **Back up this share** on every device. Follow the
[share backup and recovery steps](/docs/recovery/#back-up-a-group-share).
Keep the public group record separately: its fingerprint, member number and npub
help you check a restored share. It does not contain a private share.

To show the record again on a loaded device, choose
**Public identity → Record → Show QR**. In the companion, choose
**Load group from device** and scan every frame. **View text** on the device
returns to its fingerprint and member record. This public QR can go through the
companion; the [private share backup](/docs/recovery/#back-up-a-group-share) stays offline.

## Sign with two members

1. Load the group's public record in the companion and choose two available members.
2. Write a note or request a signature from your [Nostr client](/docs/clients/).
3. Scan the signing request on both selected devices. Review the complete action,
   event and cosigner on each device before approving.
4. Scan both devices' encrypted commitment packets into the companion, then route
   them as directed. Repeat with both encrypted share packets. Capture every
   outgoing packet before moving to the next phase.
5. Scan the final signed-event response from either participating device. The
   companion checks the original request, group identity, event and signature
   before returning it to the client.

The client still needs to publish the event. A verified signature is not a relay
receipt. After switching identities, reconnect the client and compare the full npub.

## Stop or restart

Setup has a ten-minute session limit; signing has five minutes. If a device
rejects, the session expires or you cancel, cancel on every participating device.
Start a fresh session rather than continuing with old packets.

Keep the companion open during the ceremony. Reloading it loses its coordination
state. Your private shares remain on their devices; the public group record can
reload the companion's routing information.
