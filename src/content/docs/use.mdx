---
title: Scan, review and sign
description: Use the device and companion to sign a Nostr event.
order: 1
---

## Start on battery power

Disconnect both USB cables before loading a key. The device blocks key use when
it detects external USB power or cannot confirm battery power. When powered USB is detected,
the loaded key and pending request are cleared.

Choose **Load a key** to scan your backup or enter recovery words or an nsec.
Add a passphrase if you use one and check the full npub. To make a new seed,
open **Tools → Generate a seed** and follow the [backup guide](/docs/recovery/).

## Scan a request

Open the companion and choose your public identity. When a client asks for a
signature, show its QR request to the device camera. Keep an animated QR visible
until all parts arrive.

You can also choose **Scan a QR** before loading a key. After the request arrives,
load its signing key, check the npub and choose **Use identity**. The device
resumes that request for fresh review; loading a key does not approve it or extend its expiry.

## Review on the device

Check the action, identity and affected people or events. Read the content and
open exact details when needed. A byte count or event ID cannot tell you what a
message says.

For a long text, use the full reader. If you choose to sign without seeing all
of it, that choice applies only to the current request. You still need to inspect
the recipients and other fields that grant permission.

Reject a request you do not expect. A changed request needs a new decision.

Replacing follows or relay preferences replaces the whole list. Check the entry
count and effect, and open the list details before confirming if you need to
inspect its members. The device does not compare it with your previous list.

## Return the result

Approve on the device, then scan its response with the companion. The companion
checks the event and signature before returning it to the client. The client
publishes the event. A signature alone does not confirm publication.

You can pause an animated response and move between its frames. Keep scanning
until the companion has all parts, then choose **Done** on the device.

Choose **Clear key** and confirm when you finish. This removes the key from the
session; it is not a resumable PIN lock. Load your backup again for another session.
