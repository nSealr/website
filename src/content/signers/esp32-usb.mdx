Daily-use connected touch signer with USB/display review and bridged NIP-46 inputs. **One universal Rust firmware** runs across the curated Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD family, hardened with Secure Boot v2 + Flash Encryption and no discrete secure element — many devices, one firmware.

## Hardware family

The universal firmware targets four curated boards, distinguished by display size and role:

- **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8 V2** — 240×320 IPS, the **primary** reference board.
- **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.69** — 240×280, the **compact** board.
- **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.0** — 240×320, the **standard** board.
- **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5B** — 320×480, the **premium** board.

## Boundaries

- The companion handles **NIP-46 bridge decisions** via `nsealr nip46 decide`; the device receives already-decrypted, validated signing requests.
- USB transport uses bounded frames with **request-bound capture checks**. Bring-up is covered by `nsealr serial-line exchange`.
- Smoke evidence: **companion-to-device serial smoke**, **sign-event-disabled smoke**, **firmware protocol evidence**, **Unicode fallback** tracking.
- `signing_disabled` until hardening gates pass; approvals are bound to `approval_digest`.

## Secret lifecycle

Unlike the QR vaults, the Key holds a **persistent secret** in encrypted flash (Flash Encryption). The seed is generated on device or imported from BIP-39 words. A **device PIN** — firmware-enforced and rate-limited — gates use of the key. **Backup-at-creation** exports BIP-39 words plus a SeedQR on the trusted display, one-time, at seed creation. An optional **BIP-39 passphrase** is supported.
